# Oral photo sourcing guide

The Individual Oral stimulus photos in `public/images/oral/` are currently AI-generated renders or placeholder scenes, and several do not match their prompt. Replace them with real, licensed photos.

## Rules
- Use only CC0, public-domain, CC BY, or CC BY-SA images, or Unsplash/Pexels photos (free to use, credit the photographer).
- Save each as `public/images/oral/<name>.jpg` at about 1024px wide, keeping the same name, so `src/lib/data.ts` needs no change.
- Add a matching entry to `src/lib/oralImageCredits.ts` (author, licence, source page). The photo credit then appears under the photo.
- Check the photo matches the scene description, not just the theme.
